**Real tools for real work.**

We build for the people who make, supply, and keep things running: tradespeople, fabricators, mills, contractors, construction suppliers, and tourism operators.

warre & vavasour is an independent AI laboratory in Canmore, Alberta. We turn practical problems into tools people can use on the floor, in the field, in the shop, and on the site. Success means more work won, more skill developed, and new business your people can deliver.

## What we do

### Find public work before the deadline

[WorkspaceAlberta](https://github.com/WarreAndVavasour/WorkspaceAlberta) brings CanadaBuys and Alberta Purchasing Connection into one place. Describe your trade, your town, and what you can do. Search live opportunities, check closing dates, compare fit, and get a short daily bid brief.

Start with a question like: **“I run a welding shop in Red Deer. What public work fits us, and what closes this week?”**

Search and daily briefs are free. Tender review and bid-room tools are available through Pro. [See the tools and connection guide →](https://www.warreandvavasour.com/workspacealberta)

### Give business ideas a place to become real

The [AI Terminal](https://www.warreandvavasour.com/terminal) is a supported physical workspace installed in the owner's office for a fixed term, always on and privately connected. The [WorkspaceAlberta harness](https://github.com/WarreAndVavasour/workspacealberta-harness) is the working terminal behind it. It connects tools, works with files, and produces documents and other usable artifacts.

Bring the new product you've been thinking about, the service your team could offer, or the awkward task nobody has had time to fix. We install the workspace, get to know the business, and stay alongside the work. We call it the family doctor model of support.

### Bring us your million-dollar problem

You know the idea that could be worth a million dollars; you just don't have the bandwidth to build it. [Bring it to us.](https://www.warreandvavasour.com/million) We install a Terminal on site at no cost, train one key operator, set a timeline, and work the problem with you for that fixed term.

### Teach people to use the tools on their own work

[Desk Literacy](https://www.warreandvavasour.com/training) is live, on-site AI training for owners, office managers, estimators, and coordinators. We work through real files, public tenders, decision briefs, and the office pile. The aim is a desk that works on Monday.

### Turn archives into useful landscape stories

Our [tourism and landscape work](https://www.warreandvavasour.com/tourism) turns handwritten journals, field notes, photographs, and sketch maps into searchable records, maps, and stories operators can use.

Reading an old expedition journal and reading a current tender share a practical task: turn a difficult record into something a person can act on. **One capability, two centuries of work.**

## How we build

The person doing the work comes first. The intelligence belongs in the tool, out of their way. We test ideas in the lab and bring working tools into businesses, with Canadian infrastructure and local deployment where the work allows.

The lab is allowed to be strange; the tools must be plain.
